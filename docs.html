Getting started

HeeHawDay is a farm game set on Elsberry Riding & Farm, the trail-ride and petting-zoo farm out on Grady Rd in Rockmart, Georgia. You grow crops, grind them into feed, care for the animals, and fill orders from birthday parties, Date Night couples and trail-ride groups. The farm's mascot is HeeHaw the donkey, who lives in his own pen by the pond.

You start at level 1 with 60 coins, a few bags of wheat and corn, some feed, and 18 field plots. Your progress saves in your browser automatically, and crops and animals keep working while you're away.

Controls

Move around

Drag the map to look around. Scroll, or pinch on a phone, to zoom in and out.

Do things

Tap fields, animals and buildings. Tap and drag across fields to plant or harvest many at once.

Buttons

Help, Levels, Animals, Barn, Mill and Orders sit in the bottom corner. A red badge means something is ready.

Crops

  1. Tap an empty plot and pick a seed. If you're out of that crop, you can buy seed with coins.
  2. Keep tapping or dragging across empty plots to plant more of the same crop. Press Done when you're finished.
  3. When the crop is ripe, drag across the plots to harvest them in one swipe.

Every seed you plant comes back as 2, so always keep a little of each crop to replant.

Animals

The animals are the ones that live at the real farm: the riding string, the barn and coop, and the petting zoo. Tap a hungry animal to feed it (its thought bubble shows which feed it wants). When the timer finishes, tap it again to collect. Every collection earns 3 stars.

Tap an animal and press ✎ to rename it, or rename everyone at once from the Animals button or the farmhouse.

Feed Mill

Animals don't eat crops straight from the field. Tap the Feed Mill (the building with the spinning blades) to grind crops into feed. It works on up to 3 batches at a time, one after another. Collect finished batches from the mill for 2 stars each.

Orders

The Order Board by the road always has 3 customers: birthday parties, Date Night couples, horse-camp kids, neighbors on Grady Rd and more. Each one lists the goods they want and what they'll pay in coins and stars.

  • Green chips mean you have enough of that item. When every chip is green, press Deliver.
  • Don't like an order? Press Skip. A new customer pulls up the drive in 15 seconds.
  • Orders only ask for things you've unlocked, and pay more as you add pricier goods like pies and cobbler.

Levels and unlocks

Stars fill the bar at the top of the screen. Each level needs 15 × level² stars (15 for level 1, 60 for level 2, 135 for level 3, and so on). The higher you go, the more of the farm opens up.

Tips

  • Plant wheat and corn first. They feed almost everything, and both grow fast.
  • Keep the Feed Mill busy. Hungry animals earn nothing.
  • Ponies and trail horses pay coins directly, which is the quickest way to afford seed.
  • Skip orders that need something you can't make yet instead of letting them sit.
  • Check the Levels button to plan ahead. The next unlock also shows under the star bar.
